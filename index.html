About me

ECE graduate, hands-on builder, always learning.

I am Amrut Hanamapure, an Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) graduate from City Engineering College, Bengaluru (2023–2026). I have a strong interest in core electronics and emerging technologies, with skills in VLSI design basics, Verilog, Embedded Systems, Circuit Design, Python programming, and PCB Designing & Fabrication using KiCad.

I have hands-on industry exposure: I worked in production and quality testing at Avalon Technologies, and I completed an Embedded Systems internship at VisionAstraa EV Academy, where I built an STM32-based Li-ion Battery Management System. Before that, I completed Robotics Training at Govt. Tool Room & Training Centre (GTTC), Belagavi, gaining experience in robot assembly, programming, and troubleshooting.

I have completed certifications in IoT & Its Applications and participated in a workshop on Next-Gen Embedded Solutions using ARM Cortex. Outside of coursework, I have built many electronics and embedded projects for junior college students, which sharpened both my practical skills and the way I explain technical ideas.

I am a dedicated and motivated learner, focused on building strong technical expertise and applying my knowledge to real-world engineering challenges. I am actively seeking entry-level opportunities in core electronics to grow through projects and industry experience.
